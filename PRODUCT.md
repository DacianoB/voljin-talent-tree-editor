# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Chosen for the easiest local editing and testing.

## Users

Players who want to inspect and customize the Vol'jin talent tree outside the game.

## Product Purpose

Recreate the selected Vol'jin class/spec talent tree as an editable builder. Success means users can move, add, edit, and remove talents, then save or share their altered tree.

## Positioning

Unlike a read-only build calculator, this tool edits the structure and content of the talent tree itself.

## Operating Context

Desktop-first browser editor with a usable compact/mobile layout. Users switch into edit mode, manipulate nodes and connections, and share a URL containing the saved tree.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only the selected Vol'jin class/spec is in scope.
- View mode supports allocating and refunding points.
- Edit mode supports moving, creating, editing, connecting, and deleting talents.
- Saves use browser storage; shares use URL state and require no backend.
- The referenced Ascension page is the visual and structural reference, not a source of ownership claims.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the dark, ornate fantasy talent-tree character and the selected Vol'jin identity from the supplied reference.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the exact Ascension builder URL and selected build. No proprietary source files or original art assets were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep tree editing direct and visible.
- Preserve a clear separation between allocating talents and editing the tree.
- Make every saved change recoverable without an account.
- Prefer native browser behavior and a dependency-free implementation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-focusable controls, readable contrast, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion support.
